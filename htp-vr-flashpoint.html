📖 The Playbook ▼
🎯 Objective
1
Six colored targets, fixed in place for the whole game — same position, same color, from start to finish.
2
Watch a sequence flash, then tap the targets back in the same order.
3
Get it right and the next level is one target longer — but a genuinely brand new sequence, never the one you just learned.
⭐ What Makes It Different
→
Chain Reaction replays the same growing sequence every level — this one never does. Every level is a fresh read, with zero benefit from what you memorized last time.
→
There's still no timer on your taps — this is pure accuracy, just without the comfort of repetition.
🏆 Standings
🥇
Every run gets tracked on The Standings — the longest sequence you read correctly, ranked against every other player.
💡 Tip
Don't try to memorize — read and react. There's nothing to recall from the last level here, so trust what you just watched, not what you remember.
