👁️ WATCH THE CHAIN
LEVEL 1
📖 The Playbook ▼
🎯 Objective
1
Five colored targets, fixed in place for the whole game — same position, same color, from start to finish.
2
Watch the chain light up, then tap the targets back in the same order.
3
Get it right and the chain grows by one — the whole thing replays, plus a new target on the end.
⭐ No Clock
→
There's no timer on your taps — this one is pure accuracy, not speed under pressure.
→
One wrong tap and the chain breaks — your score is how far it made it before that happened.
🏆 Standings
🥇
Every run gets tracked on The Standings — your longest chain, ranked against every other player.
💡 Tip
Learn the board's colors and positions early — since they never move, real spatial memory is what carries a long chain, not luck.
