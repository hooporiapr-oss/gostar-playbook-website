📖 The Playbook ▼
🎯 Objective
1
The target cycles through colors, one after another, to a steady beat.
2
The instant it turns red — tap it.
3
Tap on any other color and it costs you. Restraint matters as much as speed.
⭐ The Run
→
The run ends after 25 reds have appeared — that's the whole game, start to finish.
→
Fixed at 100 BPM — one steady tempo, every run, so your best score is always a fair comparison.
→
The target also moves around the screen — you're tracking both color and position at once.
🏆 Standings
🥇
Every run gets tracked on The Standings — your best score, ranked against every other player.
💡 Tip
Don't chase the target with your eyes the whole time — watching the color is what matters. Where it sits is secondary.
