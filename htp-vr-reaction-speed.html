📖 The Playbook ▼
🎯 Objective
1
A cell lights up somewhere on screen — no warning, no countdown.
2
Tap it as fast as you possibly can.
3
No colors to read, no deadline pressing on you — just how quickly you react.
⭐ Scoring
→
25 targets, back to back — that's the whole run.
→
Every reaction is scored on raw speed, then converted to its own beats-per-minute equivalent — the faster you react, the higher the number.
→
Your average BPM across all 25 targets is what lands you on the board.
🏆 Standings
🥇
Every run gets tracked on The Standings — your best score, ranked against every other player.
💡 Tip
Rest your eyes near the center of the screen between targets, not fixed on one spot — targets can appear anywhere.
