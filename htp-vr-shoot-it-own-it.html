📖 The Playbook ▼
🎯 Objective
1
An arrow flashes once in the center — then it's gone.
2
Find the button showing that same direction and tap it.
3
The four buttons reshuffle their positions every single round — so you're reading the arrow fresh every time, never relying on muscle memory.
⭐ Scoring
▶
Speed and accuracy both count — a fast, correct tap scores higher than a slow one.
▶
A full run plays out over a fixed period — every hit adds up, every miss holds you back.
🏆 Standings
🥇
Every run gets tracked on The Standings — tap any player's row to see their full shot-by-shot breakdown.
💡 Tip
Don't memorize where a direction "usually" sits — the buttons genuinely move every round. Read the arrow, then scan.
