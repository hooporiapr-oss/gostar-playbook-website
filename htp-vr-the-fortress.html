👁️ WATCH THE FORTRESS
QUARTER 1
📖 The Playbook ▼
🎯 Objective
1
Five basketballs, set in a real 2-1-2 zone inside the key — fixed in place for the whole game.
2
Watch the chain light up, then tap the balls back in the same order.
3
Get it right and the chain grows by one — the whole thing replays, plus a new ball on the end.
🏀 Four Quarters
→
Each quarter is its own chain, starting fresh — build it as far as you can before a wrong tap ends that quarter.
→
Play all four, and your final score is the total across every quarter combined.
🏆 Standings
🥇
Every run gets tracked on The Standings — tap any player's row to see their own quarter-by-quarter breakdown.
💡 Tip
Learn the zone's colors and positions early — since they never move, real spatial memory is what carries a long chain, not luck.
